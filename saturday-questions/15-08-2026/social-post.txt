Saturday Questions: Recursion

Can you solve a problem by reducing it to a smaller version of itself?

This week’s Saturday Questions contain five progressive Python recursion challenges:

- Sum from 1 to n
- Trace the recursive call stack
- Reverse a string recursively
- Find the maximum value without a loop
- Generate all binary strings using recursive backtracking

Start by identifying the base case. Then decide how every recursive call moves closer to it.

Attempt the questions, test your solutions and share your approach in the comments.
