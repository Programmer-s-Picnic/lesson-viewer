Quick start

  1. Enter or choose the subject. You can type any subject, such as Varanasi, History, Travel or a custom lesson topic.
  2. For an educational reel, choose a lesson template or add slides individually.
  3. For a picture reel, use Photo Reel. Ten pictures are recommended for a short reel, and up to 100 are allowed.
  4. Edit captions, durations, colours and animations in Slides and editing.
  5. Add optional narration, background music and web camera.
  6. Preview, then export the WebM video.

Photo Reel — 10 recommended, 100 maximum

  1. Select up to 100 pictures. They are converted into image slides in the order selected.
  2. For short-form reels, 10 pictures are recommended.
  3. The default is 5 seconds per picture. Larger photo sets create longer videos, so reduce the duration per picture when appropriate.
  4. Selections above 10 show an estimated video duration before the reel is created.
  5. If more than 100 pictures are chosen, only the first 100 are used.
  6. Choose Cover or Contain and an animation style before selecting the pictures.
  7. Use Randomize photo animations whenever you want a fresh combination.

Each picture can have three independent kinds of animation: an entrance, continuous picture motion, and an exit animation. Options include left/right/top/bottom slides, diagonal movement, zooms, rotates, wipes, fades, flips, pop, Ken Burns zoom and slow pans.

Creating and editing slides

Use the slide buttons for a hook, explanation, spoken text, code, question, answer, image or call to action (CTA). The selected slide appears in the editor.

  • Move: Use ↑ and ↓ to change the slide order.
  • Duplicate: Select ⧉ to make a copy.
  • Delete: Select × and confirm.
  • Code: Long code moves upward without shrinking its code font.
  • Image: Use Contain to show the complete picture or Cover to fill the available area.

Narration, speech and music

  1. Select a slide and type the words to be spoken in Narration and highlighted words.
  2. For synthesized narration, choose a voice and speed. When recording it, choose Entire Screen and enable Share system audio.
  3. For microphone narration, keep Remove background noise selected and record normally.
  4. You can also upload an existing narration audio file.

Background music loops through the video. Keep its volume low enough that speech remains clear.

Using the web camera

  1. Open Web camera and select Start camera.
  2. Allow camera permission in the browser.
  3. Choose its corner, size, frame shape and mirror setting.
  4. Leave the camera running while previewing and exporting.

Preview, cover and video export

Use ◀ and ▶ to inspect individual slides. Preview plays the complete reel, including photo entrance and exit animations.

  • Download cover saves the currently selected slide as a PNG.
  • Vertical Reel — 1080 × 1920: for Shorts and Reels.
  • Full-screen Video — 1920 × 1080: for regular YouTube videos.

Saving and reopening projects

The browser keeps a local working copy automatically. Use Save project to download a JSON backup and Open project to continue later.

Troubleshooting

Pictures are slow to load
The Photo Reel mode scales pictures down for reel use. Large sets such as 50–100 photographs can still take some time and memory to prepare.
Picture is cropped
Choose Contain for the complete photograph. Cover deliberately crops edges to fill the video area.
Camera or microphone does not start
Run the app through HTTPS or localhost and allow browser permission.
Synthesized narration is silent
Choose Entire Screen and enable Share system audio when recording synthesized speech.
Export is slow
The app renders the video in real time. Keep the tab active while rendering.
