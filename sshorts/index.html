User's Guide

Photo Reel

Ten pictures are recommended for short reels. Up to 100 pictures are allowed. Each picture can have an entrance, continuous motion and an exit animation.

Camera / video overlay

Choose Live web camera or Looping video file. A selected video plays muted and loops continuously across all slides and in the exported video. Position, size, circular frame and mirror settings apply to both.

Audio

Record microphone narration, upload narration, record synthesized speech, or add background music.

Export

Choose vertical or landscape. The video is rendered in real time to WebM.
